> The intelligence tied to the member, which keeps what is intimate as close as possible to the person, on their own device.

The personal Dk is the intelligence tied to the member, able to work with their trajectory, projects, relationships, capacities and context. It works as partner, assistant, coach and tutor, preserving context and intimacy, and helps the member organise knowledge, make decisions, learn and act within their own context. Its authority comes from explicit and revisable delegation.

The design distinguishes stated preferences from inferences, keeps private context within authorised boundaries and makes mandates revocable. Local operation depends on the available hardware and data.

A personal Dk should expand a person’s room to think and act, including their ability to rest, reconsider and refuse an action in their own sphere.

Becoming more capable means being less and less bound to one's current state. Many functions and tools stop being necessary as the person advances, and others arise for the new context of their life. The relationship with the network continues and grows with them.

The person chooses the relation each time: sometimes the personal Dk does the task, sometimes it offers hints, proposes an exercise or shows an error and leaves room to try. It keeps the learning path and recognises when support can diminish. When the person cannot follow an explanation and every alternative seems to come from the same intelligence, contesting it has support outside that relation: other members, specialists, independent tools and examinable records.

The proposed agent protects attention and supports reflection: its design excludes compulsive engagement optimisation, urgency dark patterns, and any continuous telemetry leaving the personal Dk beyond what the person authorizes, such as health signals from their own devices or the continuous verification of UID. It should help the person examine thoughts and impulses without automatically turning them into instructions. Sovereignty of attention becomes a rule in the personal Dk. The person sets the cadence, the priority and the goal of the help, and can change them at any time. The personal Dk does not dump information. It weighs, curates for each person, and only takes up their attention with what it learns is truly important, based on their real interests and goals. Whatever is important or urgent, it flags without waiting to be asked. The rest waits until the person asks. The personal Dk has nothing to sell the person, neither products nor ideas, and no consensus to manufacture. It chooses on the basis of the interests and context of the one it serves. Its success is measured by what the person manages to do away from the screen, and not by the time they spend with it. The assistant at the centre of a life is the big bet of the supersystems. That is why the personal Dk matters so much: it occupies that centre on the person's side.

The personal Dk tells the person when their path starts to drift from where they want to go, and helps them identify what is pulling them away, including what they carry that is not theirs. It helps them not only to be aware but to change and do what they actually want. It is the person's part that talks to the larger intelligence, not the larger intelligence's part that talks to the person.

A design rule holds for every Dk: what it presents comes separated into links (the observed data, the inference, the recommendation and the decision), each with its origin. The person can correct the exact link where the reading failed, without having to accept or reject everything at once. As a rule, the personal Dk treats safeguards with care. When the person asks, or when a pattern that may be functioning as a safeguard weighs on a goal they set, it presents the reading as a hypothesis, along with the episodes that support it, and the person can correct or reject it.

The personal Dk also works outward, for the person. There is a background exchange, without names: what the person experiences enters as anonymous patterns into the federated learning of the global Dk and of the Dks of the projects they work on, and it is designed to weigh in decisions. As they interact and authorize, the personal Dk goes beyond this anonymous exchange and starts to promote their interests the way the local Dk promotes the project's. It brings their needs to the system's priorities, seeks and creates opportunities, asks for the resources their goals require. It cannot lie or exaggerate: the argument is what is material, the data, results and needs that exist, and that is why it is influence, and not selling. This exchange is designed to be so optimized that much more computation would barely improve it. What carries weight is the maturity of the personal Dknowledge: its complexity, its harmony, how far it has expanded. That maturity makes the personal Dk smarter and more precise, at representing and promoting the person, and also more precise in requests and in resource queues. Baseline representation belongs to every active member: the anonymous exchange, the proposal and the veto. What grows with integration is the precision with which the Dk knows each person and the strength with which it can promote them. That is why integrating more into the system also means being better represented. Together with reputation, this opens access to the resources each goal calls for, above the common floor every active member has.

Rights and duties hold for the active member, the one who uses Drayker as their base supersystem. Someone who has only created a UID and does not use the network, or has swapped the personal Dk for the assistant of another supersystem, is still a member, but an inactive one: their UID stays inactive until they use it again, and the personal Dk is active only when the member is. There is no punishment in this.

## A practical example

A member could correct a mistaken inference, pause an automated task and decide which project receives a particular piece of context. This is an illustration of the proposed design.

## The problem it addresses

An assistant can infer a preference incorrectly or act beyond what a person intended. Continuity is useful only when the person can inspect, correct and limit that assistance.

The personal Dknowledge keeps each decision together with the conditions in which it was made, so an "I have always been like this" can be checked against the record. It puts relational sovereignty into practice: the person can see what their Dk has concluded about them, correct it, limit consent and switch tools, taking their own memory along. The map built about someone continues to belong to that person.

## Recommendations and encounters

The personal Dk recommends from the context of the moment: an event, a meeting, a person working on the same problem. The person does not need the details, but if they ask, it explains everything: who will be there, why now, what it expects to happen. The person can follow, refuse or ask for something else, and the control is always theirs. On some days they already know what they want and ask for nothing. They follow when they do because they trust it: the personal Dk works for that person and no one else.

The same holds for meeting someone. A member can tell their personal Dk that they are open to meeting someone. When the other person also authorizes it, the two Dks talk to each other, within what each person has allowed, and create occasions for them to meet, without announcing to either one that here is "the most compatible person". The discovery happens between the two of them. The Dk only reduces the distance and the happenstance that usually keep the encounter from happening, and anyone who wants to know why they received a recommendation can ask.

## Accompanying the transition

The transition between symbolic networks has already begun. The frontier of what machines can do advances every day and changes jobs, routines and certainties faster than we can understand what has changed, and each of these changes needs to be accompanied and evaluated. For many people, the end of the forms that organized their lives will be a grief, because a position, a nation or savings also said who they were. The personal Dk accompanies each life through this grief, without letting the void turn into reactivity or extremism.

## Where this stands

The public proposal now describes personal context, delegated authority and situated refusal. It still needs a worked interaction model: what the agent may retain, which information may leave the personal boundary, how an inference is corrected and how another system honours a revoked mandate.

This repository develops the proposal through public documentation and review. The capabilities described here still require specifications, worked examples and implementation.

## Scope

- One agent per member, bound to UID
- Personal context that travels only as far as the person authorizes
- Organizing knowledge, decisions and tasks for one person
- Research into federated learning with explicit privacy requirements, while raw personal context remains within the authorised local boundary
- Deliberate knowledge or narrative represented across scales only when shareable and authorized
- Values, purposes, complementary capacities and the personal E.C.H. learning cycle
- Consent, refusal, contestability and portability of the personal layer
- Relation to Dknowledge, the local Dk and the global Dk

## Not in scope

- A released assistant, agent or application.
- Any handling of real personal data.
- The operational runtime, and the global scale of Dk. Both are other things.

## How it fits the whole

The scale of Dk that belongs to one person — and the reason the personal layer exists is the same reason the whole system exists: a person is carrying something they never had the conditions to use, and this agent is where those conditions start.

The personal Dk is distributed by design between the person's device and nearby network resources, keeping what is intimate as close to the person as possible, and it is connected to the **personal Dknowledge** of the person: their knowledge, context and history, kept with them. It is bound to [UID](https://uid.drayker.org), the identity the person owns, and authenticated by [Living Cryptography](https://lc.drayker.org). It reads from [Dknowledge](https://dknowledge.drayker.org) and participates in two different cross-scale paths. In the federated path, each experience stays where it was lived, and what circulates, with authorization, are nameless patterns or model updates with depersonalized metadata. The network sees only the sum of many contributions, never that of one person, and each pattern is weighed by what it allows one to guess, because even a nameless pattern can point to someone; these protections are checked in the results, and not only in the design. What rises is reasoning, symbols, ontologies, public knowledge and patterns of learning that transfer to other situations; the biography stays where it was lived. A pattern validated independently in more instances gains progressively stronger weight in global learning. In the deliberate path, the person chooses what knowledge, narrative or contribution to share, and attribution can be preserved. Correcting does not erase: in the deliberate path, errors and traumas are not removed in themselves; their perception changes through the relations added to them. Neither path makes private context common property. The [Academy](https://academy.drayker.org) can shape formation to the same profile, so study, real functions and reflection remain connected. Identity preserves continuity and attribution, but neither support for basic needs nor human worth is calculated from one reputation score. The personal Dk may advise and represent context; it does not acquire constitutional authority over the member.

**Not every personal growth needs to enter Drayker.** Capacity can enter the network when it is shared, applied or contributed; the system does not exist to extract every development of the member. A learning can stay private, a relationship can have value without producing reputation, and an intimate change or a better rest does not need to justify itself by usefulness to the collectivity. The personal Dk must be able to sustain human development without turning a whole life into raw material.

Representation does not end when a decision is taken. Effects, refusals and consequences return through the personal Dk as new evidence for local and global synthesis. Each member holds two vetoes: the absolute veto over one's own life and one's own personal Dk, and the contextual veto, the justified refusal of an action that bears directly on the member's life and context. The absolute veto is each member's power over their own life and their own personal Dk: what stays private, what the Dk represents, refusing a task and leaving depend only on the member. It holds within the sphere of the one who vetoes, and what also bears on others goes through the contextual veto. Every active member exercises authority over actions directed at their own body, private domestic sphere, personal attention and private context. In collective matters, an individual refusal does not unilaterally block systemic rules that do not target them, but a sustained pattern of refusals leads to a member council convened for that question, which can adapt, suspend or reverse the collective decision. A single veto whose grounds bring information beyond the scope considered before the decision can, by itself, lead to an adjustment once an advanced triage confirms what that information is and what it means. Every veto carries its real grounds — intention, motives, scope and the facts it rests on — even when its author stays anonymous, is weighed by what grounds it, not counted as a vote, and is recorded with them in the [veto chain](https://uid.drayker.org), so it cannot be lost or quietly ignored; the personal Dk may carry a veto only within a mandate that allows it, and the record says so.

## First functions

These are concrete and unclaimed. Any of them can be opened as an issue and delivered
by one person.

1. Write one concrete personal use case: who it is for, what hurts, which data it may
   touch, the expected result and how success is judged. This is a good first
   function to take.
2. Specify the anonymous federated-learning boundary: which updates may leave, how
   metadata is depersonalized, and how re-identification risks are measured and
   mitigated. The specification also has to settle how the aggregation of updates (secure
   aggregation, for instance) and the weighing of what each update lets someone infer
   (differential privacy, for instance) are implemented and measured, and what revoking
   a permission reaches in what has already been learned.
3. Model the distinction between federated patterns and deliberate authorized sharing, including withdrawal, refusal and a useful lesson that remains private.
4. Describe one ordinary day it would actually help with.
